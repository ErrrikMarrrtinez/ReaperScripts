Breath component:

Respiro-en annotations: https://github.com/ydqmkkx/Respiro-en
Only the manually annotated development CSV was used, not their neural weights.
A separate small tree classifier was fitted and exported to Lua numerical data.
Version 0.7 also uses locally supplied recordings and breath annotations for
adaptation and development checks. Those recordings are not distributed here.
